A few things worth knowing.

What gets flagged as a duplicate?

Website hostnames ignore scheme, a leading www., port and page paths. Company names normalize Unicode, case, spaces and punctuation. Shared domains or names can represent different branches, so flags need human review. Company suffixes are not guessed away.

What is included in the cleaned CSV?

The first complete, valid, formula-free representative per company name or website. It needs a company name and an absolute HTTP(S) website. A Source URL column is optional; if supplied, its values must also be complete and valid. An incomplete earlier row does not suppress a later complete representative.

Which files work?

UTF-8 with optional BOM, comma/semicolon/tab separators, quoted commas and newlines, and empty values. Limits: 2,097,152 bytes, 2,000 data records, 100 columns, and 128 UTF-8 bytes per heading. Ambiguous headers and malformed rows are rejected. Blank physical lines are ignored.

Does a format pass mean a lead is verified?

No. This tool checks data quality, not whether a company exists, a website works or a prospect intends to buy. Domain-only values are flagged rather than silently adding a scheme. Original values stay unchanged in memory; exports use safe quoting and formula escaping.
